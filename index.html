Why Do Disasters Happen?

Understanding causes can help communities prepare.

🌍

Natural Processes

Earthquakes, volcanic eruptions, storms, and floods can result from natural processes.

🏭

Human Activity

Unsafe work practices, pollution, and accidents can cause serious emergencies.

🏘️

Vulnerability

Buildings, infrastructure, and emergency plans can affect how badly a community is impacted.

🌦️

Weather Conditions

Heavy rain, strong winds, heat, and drought can increase certain disaster risks.

📢

Early Warnings

Official alerts can give people time to prepare, move to safety, or follow instructions.

🤝

Community Support

Training, planning, and helping one another can make communities more resilient.

Step by step

Before, During and After

Choose a disaster to see what to do in each stage.

⏪ Before

  • Secure heavy furniture to the walls.
  • Find safe spots: under a strong table, away from windows.
  • Practice drop, cover and hold on.

⚠️ During

  • Drop, cover and hold on.
  • Stay away from windows and heavy objects.
  • Do not use the elevator.

⏩ After

  • Expect aftershocks.
  • Check yourself and others for injuries.
  • Leave damaged buildings and follow official instructions.

⏪ Before

  • Know your area's flood risk.
  • Prepare an emergency kit and documents.
  • Agree on a family meeting place on higher ground.

⚠️ During

  • Move to higher ground right away.
  • Do not walk or drive through flood water.
  • Follow evacuation orders.

⏩ After

  • Return home only when authorities say it is safe.
  • Avoid flood water; it can be contaminated.
  • Do not touch electrical equipment if wet.

⏪ Before

  • Never leave fires or cigarettes unattended outdoors.
  • Know evacuation routes from your area.
  • Keep emergency numbers at hand.

⚠️ During

  • Call 112 or 101 immediately.
  • Leave early when told to evacuate.
  • Cover nose and mouth to limit smoke.

⏩ After

  • Do not return until it is declared safe.
  • Watch for hot spots and smoke.
  • Follow health advice about smoke exposure.
A part of our history

The 1988 Spitak Earthquake

Armenia · 7 December 1988

A lesson in preparedness

A powerful earthquake struck northern Armenia on 7 December 1988. Spitak and other towns and villages suffered severe destruction, and many people lost their lives.

The disaster showed the importance of safe buildings, emergency planning, rescue services, and community support.

What can we learn? Know the safety rules, follow official instructions, and take part in school emergency drills.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most important thing to do in an emergency?

Stay calm, protect yourself first, and follow instructions from emergency services.

What should an emergency kit contain?

Water, long-lasting food, first-aid supplies, a flashlight, spare batteries, important phone numbers and warm clothing. Use the checklist above to track yours.

How can I get official alerts?

Follow local authorities and emergency services, and do not trust or share unverified rumors.

Why is a family plan important?

Communication can be hard during a disaster. A plan with a meeting place and contact numbers helps everyone know what to do.
